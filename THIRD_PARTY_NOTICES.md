## League of Legends Wiki-derived data

Files under `data/champions/` contain normalized factual values derived from
pages identified by each file's `source` field. The synchronization tool uses
the League of Legends Wiki at https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us.

Downloaded Wiki source text is only a local build cache. It is excluded from
the Git repository and is not included in release packages. Use of the Wiki
and its content remains subject to the Wiki's terms and the rights of its
